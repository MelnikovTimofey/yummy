Icon-only action in dense rows (edit, more, remove) — always with a label for a11y.

```jsx
<MasterIconButton label="Редактировать"><i data-lucide="pencil" /></MasterIconButton>
<MasterIconButton small label="Убрать"><i data-lucide="x" /></MasterIconButton>
```

Icons are Lucide at 15px, stroke ~1.8, `--text-muted` until hover.
