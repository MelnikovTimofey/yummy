Panel container for console content — all Мастер modules share this one surface rhythm.

```jsx
<MasterCard heading="Топ миксов недели">
  …rows…
</MasterCard>
```

12px radius, subtle raised gradient, `--shadow-md`. Two columns of these is the standard dashboard layout; don't invent alternative card treatments per module. The heading stands alone: no kicker above it, no lead line under it.
